Private by design

Vityss reads Apple Health read-only and computes your scores on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your health data, audio and location never leave your device. The only thing sent off-device is optional, anonymised crash and usage diagnostics — never your health data, name, scores, audio or location.

Vityss offers wellness insights, not medical advice — its scores are not a diagnosis and shouldn't be treated as final. Always consult a doctor about your health.
