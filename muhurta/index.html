A look inside

Private by design

Location is used to set place and timezone for Local Lahiri calculations. Birth details and charts stay on your iPhone. Muhurta does not run a cloud ephemeris or chart backend. Place search may call Google Places so you can pick a city; your kundli data is not uploaded for astrology.

Muhurta offers astrological timing and cultural calendar tools for personal wellness and tradition. It is not medical advice, not a religious authority, and not a substitute for a qualified pandit or clinician.
