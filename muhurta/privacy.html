Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Muhurta is a privacy-forward iPhone app for Local Lahiri muhurta timing, panchang, kundli and festivals. This policy explains what stays on your device and the narrow network calls used for place search.

Not medical or religious authority

Muhurta provides astrological timing and cultural calendar tools for personal use. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for a qualified clinician. It is also not a religious authority and not a substitute for guidance from a pandit or tradition you trust. Treat results as informational estimates from Local Lahiri on-device calculation.

The short version

  • Your birth details, kundli charts and muhurta results stay on your device.
  • Muhurta does not run a cloud ephemeris or chart backend. Local Lahiri calculations run on-device.
  • Location is used to set place and timezone for calculations (and for “current location” when you allow it).
  • Place search may send the text you type (and place IDs) to Google Places / Time Zone APIs so you can pick a city. Birth charts are not uploaded for astrology.
  • Muhurta does not currently ship crash or usage analytics SDKs. There is no optional off-device diagnostics product in the app today.
  • We do not track you for advertising and do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Depending on what you enter, Muhurta may store locally:

  • Name and birth date/time you provide for kundli
  • Birth place or current place (name, coordinates, timezone)
  • Preferences, local festival reminders and on-device logs for debugging on your phone

Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device. There is no Muhurta account server holding a copy of your kundli.

Location

When you grant location permission, Muhurta uses your location on-device to resolve place and timezone for Local Lahiri muhurta, panchang and festival windows. Location is not used for advertising. If permission is denied, you can still pick a place explicitly; the app does not invent a silent default city.

Place search (Google)

Birth-place and place pickers use Google Places API (New) autocomplete and place details, and may use Google’s Time Zone API after coordinates are known. Those requests include the search text or place identifiers needed to return a place. They are not used to upload your full birth chart or muhurta timeline to an astrology cloud.

Notifications

Optional festival or muhurta reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Reminder content is not sent to a Muhurta server.

No cloud ephemeris

Muhurta’s timing engine is designed to compute Local Lahiri results on-device. We do not operate a Muhurta-owned backend that stores your charts or streams ephemeris results keyed to your birth data.

Third parties

  • Apple — App Store distribution, device APIs, and (when you purchase) In-App Purchase billing handled by Apple.
  • Google — Places / Time Zone lookups for place search only, as described above.

Children

Muhurta is not directed at children. Use requires a device and Apple ID appropriate for the App Store age rating.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will refresh the “last updated” date on this page.
